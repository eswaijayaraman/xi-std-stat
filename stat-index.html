Measures of Dispersion

Master Mean Absolute Deviation, Variance, Standard Deviation, and Coefficient of Variation with interactive calculation tools and step-by-step solutions.

$\sigma^2$

1. Mean Absolute Deviation (M.A.D.)

M.A.D. for Ungrouped Data

Absolute deviation about Mean ($\bar{x}$) or Median ($M$):

$$M.A.D.(\bar{x}) = \frac{\sum |x_i - \bar{x}|}{n}, \quad M.A.D.(M) = \frac{\sum |x_i - M|}{n}$$

M.A.D. for Grouped / Frequency Distribution

Discrete / Continuous Frequency Distribution ($N = \sum f_i$):

$$M.A.D.(\bar{x}) = \frac{\sum f_i |x_i - \bar{x}|}{N}, \quad M.A.D.(M) = \frac{\sum f_i |x_i - M|}{N}$$

2. Variance ($\sigma^2$) & Standard Deviation ($\sigma$)

Direct Method (Continuous Series)

$$\sigma^2 = \frac{\sum f_i (x_i - \bar{x})^2}{N} = \frac{\sum f_i x_i^2}{N} - \left(\frac{\sum f_i x_i}{N}\right)^2$$

Short-cut Method ($d_i = x_i - A$)

$$\sigma^2 = \frac{\sum f_i d_i^2}{N} - \left(\frac{\sum f_i d_i}{N}\right)^2$$

Step-deviation ($y_i = \frac{x_i - A}{h}$)

$$\sigma^2 = \frac{h^2}{N^2} \left[ N \sum f_i y_i^2 - (\sum f_i y_i)^2 \right]$$

3. Coefficient of Variation (C.V.) & Comparison

Formula for C.V.

Used to compare variability or consistency of distributions:

$$C.V. = \frac{\sigma}{\bar{x}} \times 100 \quad (\bar{x} \neq 0)$$

Key Principles

  • • Higher C.V.: Greater variability / Less consistent / Less stable.
  • • Lower C.V.: Less variability / More consistent / More stable.
  • • Equal Means ($\bar{x}_1 = \bar{x}_2$): Distribution with greater SD $\sigma$ has greater variability.
